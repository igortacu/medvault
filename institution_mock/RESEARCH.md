# Moldovan medical documents — research notes for the mock generator

What real documents issued by medical institutions in the Republic of Moldova
contain, so the synthetic data in `fixtures/` looks plausible. Everything below
describes **structure and fields** in our own words; no form text is reproduced.
The generator (`generate_fixtures.py`) follows these notes; every generated PDF is
watermarked `MOSTRĂ — date sintetice`.

> Caveat: most primary sources are scans, procurement lists or draft regulations.
> Where a fact could not be confirmed it is marked *(unconfirmed)* and the generator
> treats it as a plausible convention, not a rule.

## 1. The form system

- Primary medical records use numbered statistical forms with an `/e` suffix
  (`003/e`, `025/e`, `027/e`, `063/e`, `083/e`, `086/e`, …). Most of them were
  approved by **Ordinul Ministerului Sănătății nr. 828 din 31.10.2011** (cited as the
  legal basis of nearly every form in public-procurement lists of IMSP clinics);
  a few later forms cite other orders (e.g. vaccine-stock forms, order 1265/2013).
  [1] [2] [3]
- Every form carries in its top block: *Republica Moldova*, *Ministerul Sănătății*,
  the institution name (an IMSP — *Instituție Medico-Sanitară Publică* — or a
  private provider), and the form number with the approving order. The bottom block
  has the doctor's name, signature and personal stamp (*parafa*), and, for documents
  handed to the patient, the institution stamp (*L.Ș.* — *locul ștampilei*). The
  discharge extract is stamped with both the rectangular and the triangular
  institution stamp. [4]
- Forms are printed A5 or A4 (most certificates and referrals are one A5 sheet). [2]

## 3. Diagnosis coding (CIM-10)

- Moldova codes diagnoses with **CIM-10** (ICD-10). National and institutional
  clinical protocols name the CIM-10 code of each disease, and the Ministry ordered
  the WHO COVID-19 codes U07.1/U07.2 for public and private institutions. [20] [21]
- Hospitals are paid by **DRG (CASE-MIX)**; around 70 hospitals were contracted
  this way from 2013. Coding, collection and validation of patient-level data is
  governed by the joint MS/CNAM order 397/125A of 5 April 2013 (amended 2018), and
  CNAM audits coding. [22] [23]
- In the inpatient record the **main diagnosis** is the condition that consumed the
  most resources, coded with 4 characters; up to 6 secondary diagnoses
  (complications / comorbidities actually investigated or treated) are coded the
  same way; a treatment complication cannot be the main diagnosis. [14] [24]
- Generator choice: diagnoses carry an `http://hl7.org/fhir/sid/icd-10` coding with
  the CIM-10 code and the Romanian name, plus a Romanian `text`.

## 4. Choices the generator makes from this

- Romanian content everywhere a person reads it (titles, diagnoses, conclusions,
  dosage, doctor names, ward names); FHIR keys and code systems (LOINC, ICD-10, ATC,
  `urn:medvault:*`) stay as they are.
- PDFs follow the layout in §1: header block, form number, document number and date,
  patient block, body, signature / *parafa* / *L.Ș.* placeholders. The IDNP is shown
  **masked** (last 4 digits) — the backend streams these files and the IDNP must
  never pass through MedVault (D2).
- Sick-leave certificates get a synthetic portal-style unique number and a QR
  placeholder; the reason is "boală obișnuită" or "îngrijirea copilului bolnav".
- Compensated prescriptions are flagged; the DCI and the commercial name are both
  shown. Which DCIs are compensated in the mock is synthetic — it is not the current
  CNAM list.
